# Farm Fortress architecture

`Game` owns deterministic economy/combat. Four side indexes (N/E/S/W) replace three lanes; the persisted field name `lane` remains as a side index. An enemy has distance outside its assigned wall and an approach offset. World positions use normalized coordinates with a fortress spanning0.18..0.82 on both axes. Mercenaries move along its clockwise rectangular perimeter, choose spatial targets within0.28 units and retain their own cooldown and last shot target. Patrol motion remains active in daylight; firing requires night. Defeat stops ticks. A maximum6 guards ×45 enemies bounds targeting work.

Crops yield coins and timber; three explicit construction flags enable passive timber, market sale bonus and recruitment. Wall tiers modify incoming damage at the attacked side against shared fortress strength. Global gear upgrades remain separate. Passive production uses active dt only. Tick caps dt at100ms; no wall-clock catch-up or background progression.

`BoardGeometry` maps the simulation rectangle into display pixels and lays out nine crops, three interior buildings and four fence action markers. Portrait uses a building row over the farm; landscape uses a building column. All interior bounds stay within the perimeter; native hit targets and Canvas consume the same boxes. Font-aware minimum world height preserves48dp targets; short/large-font screens scroll. World-to-pixel transforms map enemies/guards to the exact fence. Original vector sprites and native ripple buttons give an arcade feel without bitmap assets or dependencies.

`MainActivity` contains the world, compact HUD, expandable seed picker and Seeds/Build/Harvest/Night dock. Fence/Farm/Squad/Gear pages are a paused, touch-consuming overlay hiding the background from accessibility traversal. Map wall/building taps open the relevant page; purchase costs are explicit. Closing stays paused. Seed, selected wall and panel page survive rotation. Existing focus loss/Home/resume pause, immersive cutout/caption insets and document-picker flows remain.

`SaveCodec` schema2 adds enemy offsets, timber, four wall tiers, three building flags, production clock and up to6 guard states. CRC,16KiB size cap, finite/range/count/relationship checks validate input before replacement. Schema1 is explicitly decoded and migrated; old progression survives. Schema2 exports cannot be downgraded. No personal data/network access.

`FarmApp` retains one game and writer across recreation. `SaveStore` asynchronously coalesces snapshots into two alternating AtomicFile slots, selecting the newest valid revision and visibly recovering from one damaged slot. Both damaged blocks automatic overwrite until reset/import. Save on mutations, every3 active seconds and background; loss before a successful callback can include the checkpoint interval and pending I/O. Import/export uses a separate serial worker and SAF; invalid imports leave state unchanged. No private signing key is committed.
