# 0.10 state and settlement architecture

Settlement maps day to one of3 deterministic five-day chapters. It owns ring coordinates: rectangle Farm, trapezoidal Village and8-edge chamfered Castle. Game instance guard/foe/ring coordinate methods use it for combat, while FarmView uses the exact same points to draw fences, actors and courtyard. Static old square helpers remain compatibility/test utilities only. GameBoard requests layout when chapter changes; BoardGeometry permutes tile positions without shrinking48dp hit areas. Unit checks cover shape continuity, containment, geometry and resource preservation. No transition consumes the simulation RNG.

Game adds wallRanks[4], millLevel/drill/heroLevel, hero(nullable Mercenary), item counts and enhancerTime. Hero is outside the3-member mercenary list, with its own stats, healing and death. Perimeter/range and guard targeting code covers both actor classes. Enhancer scales shot strength/cadence and ticks only in Game.tick; recovery clears it. Elixir affects living crew/core, never resurrects. Mill upgrades alter the actual production period/yield; destroyed buildings still stop benefits.

Schema7 appends new state after schema6 crew health. Decode bounds provisional health before reading ranks/drills, then validates exact final maxima, actor state, stock caps and timers before accepting CRC/trailing length. Old saves initialize new fields to zero/null without a free hero or item. Legacy investment migration/refunds remain. No new permission/service/dependency.

MainActivity uses1.8s main-handler inline feedback tied to a button/token, cleared on selection/success/context changes. Unaffordability does not disable a valid upgrade; max/prerequisites still do. Bottle slots are original Drawable art, placed under transparent Merc controls on both sides of the compass. Extra top/bottom padding separates command area from global actions. Popups expose related advanced/item/hero purchases and retain modal pause/accessibility behavior. All audio remains generated from source;32s phase themes replace16s loops and preserve lifecycle/focus/mute behavior.

## Farm Fortress architecture

`Game` owns deterministic economy/combat. Four side indexes (N/E/S/W) replace three lanes; the persisted field name `lane` remains as a side index. An enemy has distance outside its assigned wall and an approach offset. Up to10 daytime Roamer records hold type, clockwise ring progress and distance; they move only on active ticks and cannot damage the farm during preparation. Starting a raid promotes their exact world positions into Enemy records, counts them against the existing45-enemy wave quota and clears the roaming list. Dawn/recovery replenish scouts. Player level shortens preparation75→30s and reinforcements4.8→1.2s over levels1→10; day still controls quota/variant/HP progression. World positions use normalized coordinates with a fortress spanning0.18..0.82 on both axes. Mercenaries move along its clockwise rectangular perimeter, choose spatial targets within0.85 units and retain their own cooldown and last shot target. Patrol motion remains active in daylight; firing requires night. Mercenaries alone damage enemies. Each guard fires16+4×training damage every max(0.5,0.85−0.06×training) seconds. Normal patrol moves0.14 sides/s; rally uses shortest perimeter travel at0.35 sides/s to spread guards along a selected border. Intruders and rallied-side threats receive priority within range. A new farm begins with one volunteer; recovery supplies one only if the squad is empty. Defeat stops ticks. A maximum6 guards ×45 enemies bounds targeting work.

Crops yield coins and timber; three explicit construction flags enable passive timber, market sale bonus and recruitment. Each border has independent health:80+40×(tier−1)+15×fortify level, or0 for an unbuilt gap. Contact/ranged wall attacks deplete only that side. At zero health, zombies can cross the gap and advance on fixed inward assault lanes. Intruders target live crops first (side-aware plot order), then buildings, then the homestead core; red hit flashes identify the affected tile. This is bounded lane combat, not free pathfinding. Existing intruders remain after repairs close a breach. Crops have40HP and buildings100HP. Zero-health crops are cleared without sale/XP/timber, leaving scorched replanted soil; zero-health buildings disable their benefit and require reconstruction. A destroyed barracks blocks new hires but retains existing guards. Target validity is rechecked before each hit, preventing duplicate destruction rewards/counters. Global gear upgrades remain separate; the core retains100+50×fortify health. Recovery restores core and invested fence tiers but retains asset losses, with a12-coin minimum for seeds. Passive production uses active dt only. Tick caps dt at100ms; no wall-clock catch-up or background progression.

`BoardGeometry` maps the simulation rectangle into display pixels and lays out nine crops, three interior buildings and four fence action markers. Portrait uses a building row over the farm; landscape uses a building column. All interior bounds stay within the perimeter; native hit targets and Canvas consume the same boxes. The farm has a capped256dp portrait/380dp landscape width and font-aware, content-sized height; extra viewport height expands wilderness rather than soil tiles. The narrow320dp layout preserves56dp side terrain. A12dp world inset keeps spawned sprites on screen. Font-aware minimum world height preserves48dp targets; short/large-font screens scroll. World-to-pixel transforms map enemies/guards to the exact fence. Original vector sprites and native ripple buttons give an arcade feel without bitmap assets or dependencies.

`MainActivity` contains the world, compact HUD, expandable seed picker and Seeds/Build/Harvest/Raid dock. Fence/Farm/Squad/Gear pages are a paused, touch-consuming overlay hiding the background from accessibility traversal. Map wall/building taps open the relevant page; purchase costs are explicit. Closing stays paused. Seed, selected wall and panel page survive rotation. Existing focus loss/Home/resume pause, immersive cutout/caption insets and document-picker flows remain.

`SaveCodec` schema5 changes the global lane field to−1(patrol) or0..3(rally) and interprets the retained weapon field as guard training. Legacy shot/focus clock fields remain format placeholders and never damage enemies. Schemas1–4 reset old firing orders and grant a volunteer only when no guard exists; schema5 reloads never duplicate it. Schema4 appends damage/ruin/loss and intruder state to schema3. Schema3 appends a bounded roaming roster to schema2, which adds enemy offsets, timber, four wall tiers, three building flags, production clock and up to6 guard states. CRC,16KiB size cap, finite/range/count/relationship checks validate input before replacement. Schema1/2/3/4 are explicitly decoded and migrated; old progression survives. Schema5 exports cannot be read by older releases. Migration initializes individual asset health from existing tiers/flags/crops without changing resources, growth, units, roaming or core health. Damage fields are finite/range/relationship validated; saved guard squads may outlive their barracks in schema4. No personal data/network access.

`FarmApp` retains one game and writer across recreation. `SaveStore` asynchronously coalesces snapshots into two alternating AtomicFile slots, selecting the newest valid revision and visibly recovering from one damaged slot. Both damaged blocks automatic overwrite until reset/import. Save on mutations, every3 active seconds and background; loss before a successful callback can include the checkpoint interval and pending I/O. Import/export uses a separate serial worker and SAF; invalid imports leave state unchanged. No private signing key is committed.

Economy actions use atomic preflight checks: timber bundle10w/12c (reject if it would exceed the resource cap); side repair+50HP/8c/4w; building repair+60HP/10c/5w; full reconstruction uses the original building price. Harvest remains the main source of coins/timber; recruitment costs and prerequisite barracks remain. UI displays repair/build/rebuild costs before the action; overlays pause the simulation.

FarmView draws ordinary small fence posts, material-specific wood pickets/stone blocks/steel rails and three partially-filled vector hearts per border from Game.fenceHeartRatio. Numeric HP remains in native labels, repair actions and accessibility descriptions, with a single compass label at large fonts. No towers or structure-origin shots are drawn. Zombies use distinct original silhouettes/details and gait derived from simulation state, so pausing freezes them and reduced motion suppresses gait. A24dp world inset keeps larger sprites and health bars in bounds.
