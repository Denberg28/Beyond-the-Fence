Beyond the Fence **0.6.0-rc.1 · Fences and Mercenaries**

- Mercenaries are the only fighters. Towers, automatic structure fire and instant focus shots are removed.
- One volunteer protects a new farm. Hire more after building barracks; train the whole crew in Gear. Rally moves guards to N/E/S/W, while Patrol returns them to their circuit.
- Upgrade Wood → Stone → Steel fences. Their health and mitigation differ; coins buy the material kit and timber supplies framing. Three heart icons at each border show full, partial or empty remaining life; exact HP is available in Fence and accessibility labels.
- Redesigned walkers, runners, armored brutes and bloated spitters have original larger silhouettes, eyes/teeth, ragged clothing and simulation-driven movement. Reduced motion keeps poses still.
- Existing breaches, crop/building destruction, harvest-funded materials/repairs/recruitment, paused construction and backup recovery remain.

Install **Preview.apk** on Android8.0+. Test-signed prerelease; export your farm before upgrading/uninstalling. Certificate conflicts can require export→uninstall→install→import. Schemas1–4 migrate to schema5. Existing rifle tiers become mercenary training; damage/resources/guard squads remain. A guardless old farm receives one volunteer once. Schema5 cannot be opened in0.5 or older; keep a pre-upgrade backup and APK for rollback.

Release gates: JVM defense/economy/damage/codec/geometry tests, builds/lint/package/signature audits, and both API26/35 exact-APK UI suites. Native evidence includes full/half/breached heart pixels, material-kit purchases, rally movement/order behavior, redesign screenshots, lifecycle/fonts and checkpoint recovery. Check VERIFICATION.txt and evidence ZIP for observed results. Physical-phone endurance, TalkBack, measured performance and production signing remain pending; this is not a stable release.
