# 0.15.0-rc.1 — Quiet water, floating gardens

The Merc shortcut is now Plant: choose one or several empty beds, or plant all affordable beds. Direct taps still plant and harvest individual beds. Uniforms remain in Crew. The three bottom spaces form a fishing ground in every settlement layout. Three successful catches per in-game day open all three as floating beds for any currently available crop. Existing floating crops keep growing across dawn, saves and upgrades; emptied beds return to water until the quota is met again. The existing 200-coin expansion now provides permanent floating beds before the quota. Hold any bottom bed to fish, including catch-and-release after the reward limit.

Fishing pauses farm time and leaves it paused on exit. Cast, wait for the ripple, and reel while it lies between the two gold rings (a 1-second catch window). Misses cost nothing and do not consume the quota. The first three catches pay 6–10 coins or, from day 10, have a 2% chance of a Common/Uncommon equipment find. Further catches release the fish with no coin, equipment or RNG rewards. Fishing uses separate saved randomness; combat/loot RNG and maximum equipment bonuses are unchanged. The quota resets only at dawn, including after save/reload and recovery.

The original Turnip/Corn/Pumpkin economics are unchanged. Carrot/day 6, Cabbage/day 12 and Strawberry/day 20 add steady options. Moonleaf/day 30 needs Spring+Fog (Ashfall in World 2), Sunpepper/day 45 needs Summer+Heat, and Rain lily/day 60 needs Autumn+Rain. Seasons rotate every 10 in-game days. Conditions apply when planting only; existing plants remain viable afterward. New crops never exceed Pumpkin net coin or timber yield per second. Gentle optional-action messages and a shorter welcome/help replace repeated instructions.

Schema 11 appends the saved fishing quota and independent RNG, accepts nine crop IDs, and reads schemas 1–10. All nine existing crop positions, growth, health, investments, equipment and world progress are retained. Old expanded gardens arrive as permanent floating beds; no crop is evicted or auto-sold. Older apps reject schema 11 exports: retain the prior APK and pre-update backup for rollback.

Required candidate checks: final debug/release unit variants, Android builds/lint, package audits, API 26/35 native regression and new fishing/planting flows, screenshots and same-certificate installation/migration. This source is a development candidate until those gates are recorded. Physical-phone endurance/audio/TalkBack/performance and human balance remain unvalidated; no stable promotion.
