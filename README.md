# 0.16.0-rc.1 — Quick planting and a separate pond

Plant now behaves like Harvest: one tap fills all affordable empty unlocked beds with the selected seed, without a chooser, toast or pause. It preserves growing/ripe crops and debits only planted seeds. Individual beds still support planting and harvesting. Seasonal/day requirements and the six-bed starting farm plus 200-coin expansion remain unchanged.

Swipe down to hide Seeds, the bottom command buttons, construction/equipment panels and ordinary popups. Show controls restores the command dock. Horizontal seed scrolling and vertical content scrolling retain priority; scroll a panel to the top before pulling it down. Buttons and Close/Back remain available. Required recovery/progression choices and arena combat retain their explicit actions.

Fishing now lives in a separate visible pond below the south fence. All nine farm positions draw ordinary soil; the bottom three open through normal Farm expansion. The three-catch daily reward cap, timed reel, separate randomness and equipment odds are unchanged. Swiping the fishing popup closes it, cancels the cast and keeps the farm paused.

Wolves stay upright rather than rotating a side-view drawing through 360 degrees. They mirror toward travel/targets, and diagonal pairs of articulated legs animate a trot. Pause and Reduced motion retain static poses. No enemy stats or damage change.

Save schema 12 reads schemas 1–11. A schema11 farm that has crops in former floating beds, or has earned its three-catch quota, retains those beds as permanent ordinary farmland on upgrade. Crop positions, growth, health, resources, equipment and progression remain intact. New fishing catches never unlock farmland. Older apps reject schema12 exports: export before updating and keep the previous APK and pre-update backup for rollback.

Required candidate checks: debug/release JVM suite, Android builds/lint, package/audio/signature audits, installed-app regression and new real-pointer planting/swipe/pond checks on API26 and35, visual wolf/pond review, and same-key release upgrade retention. Physical-phone performance, accessibility, endurance and human balance validation remain pending. This is a prerelease, not a stable promotion.

# TAG: Zombie Survival Fantasy

A small farm, a warm home, and something moving past the fence. An offline Android farming and survival game with original vector artwork, quick sessions, and no accounts, ads, purchases or internet permission.

**Published baseline 0.15.0-rc.1** adds the Plant chooser, seasonal crops, quiet fishing and three floating garden beds, on top of the medieval frontier, fantasy bestiary, illustrated arena and Ashen March. All automated prerelease and signed-upgrade gates passed; physical-phone and human balance validation remain pending.

**Current GitHub prerelease: [0.15.0-rc.1](https://github.com/Denberg28/Beyond-the-Fence/releases/tag/v0.15.0-rc.1).** Install the signed Release.apk; Preview.apk is the debuggable test build. Android 8.0+ (API 26), phones and tablets. Native Java/Canvas; no runtime dependencies. Builds on the verified0.11.1-rc.1 preview;0.12 starts a persistent private signing identity. Baseline rules: [Denberg28/Agents_md v1.1](https://github.com/Denberg28/Agents_md/blob/main/AGENTS.md). The initial rules commit is `1e9f9a0de2f926124d00c8378204c76669db3900`.

## Play · farm fortress

The game is a top-down fortress defense with original, chunky arcade artwork. Crops, a mill, a market and barracks sit inside the fence. The farm occupies a compact central area, leaving visible terrain on every side. Zombies wander outside during preparation, then approach from north, east, south and west during raids. Uniformed mercenaries patrol the perimeter and shoot nearby targets; mercenaries and a recruitable hero fight the horde. One volunteer guard starts with each new farm.

1. Open **Seeds**, select a crop, then tap soil inside the walls. Harvest ripe tiles or use Harvest to earn **coins and timber**. Start with80 coins,24 timber and6 plots; corn/pumpkins become available on days2/3; there are no player levels.
2. The compact, transparent bottom-center **N/W/E/S cross** selects a side and keeps the direct repair action (8 coins, up to50HP, no timber or pause). The center **↑** builds/upgrades only the selected fence, at the usual coin/timber price. It starts disabled until a direction is selected. Unaffordable upgrades stay tappable and briefly show their required coins/timber inside the button, with no charge. Fully maxed upgrades are disabled. Ironbound starts with4 reinforcement ranks and can unlock up to20 through the century campaign. Intact-side selection costs nothing. A thin outline marks selection; color and durability bars report damage. East/west start as gaps. Spend coins/timber to build each border, then upgrade **Palisade → Stone wall → Ironbound**. Durability bars, cracks and numeric HP show remaining fence strength; hearts have been removed. Each fence has independent health. Zombies chip it down and enter when it reaches zero; stronger tiers absorb more damage. Tap a damaged border to repair it for8 coins (+50HP, no timber). Mill sells10 timber for12 coins; Fence owns homestead fortification. Repair homestead restores the separate farm-core health.
3. Tap an interior **Mill/Shop/Camp** tile to open that building’s own features. Mill provides its construction/repairs, output upgrades and timber bundles. Farm provides market construction/repairs, selling ripe crops, planting the selected crop, irrigation and expansion. Crew (Barracks/Camp) provides its construction/repairs, recruitment, crew status, rally/patrol, mercenary drills and hero recruitment/training. One hero is separate from the3 mercenary slots; hero death requires paid replacement. Mill starts with3 production tiers and can unlock10; Barracks drills and hero training start at3 tiers and can unlock20. The timber mill produces4 timber every12 active seconds; the market adds25% to crop sale value; barracks unlock recruitment. All purchases show costs before confirmation by tapping their purchase button.
4. Open **Crew / Barracks** to hire; maximum3 mercenaries. Hiring still requires barracks, the shown coin price and6 timber. They roam the fence even during daylight and shoot zombies within range at night. No recurring wages. Breached zombies can injure and kill mercenaries. Surviving guards heal1.5HP per active preparation second. Rally N/E/S/W moves guards to defend that border; Resume perimeter patrol releases the order. Rally never deals an instant shot.
5. **Plant** beside the cross fills all affordable empty unlocked beds without pausing; the **Crew** panel upgrades crew uniforms (Workwear → Scout green → Desert kit → Urban armor → Ranger kit → Black ops). Fence owns homestead fortification; Farm owns irrigation and expansion. Uniforms visibly change armor, colors and insignia, add3 damage and20 maximum health per tier, improve firing cadence/range and reduce injuries. Each day raises the threat: preparation75→35s, reinforcement3.2→0.6s, quota10→80. Runners rush at twice Walker speed; Brutes are slow, durable fence smashers; Spitters attack from outside the fence. Each killed zombie pays **1 coin**, once; harvests and dawn rewards remain the main income. **Every zombie must be killed to end the raid**; there is no75-second despawn. Rally the short-range crew to threatened borders. Intruders destroy crops and buildings and can wipe out your farm. Defeat offers free recovery at the same day with one volunteer if the squad was lost, keeping surviving assets and upgrades; ruined crops/buildings stay lost. Recovery grants enough seed coins to reach12 if needed.
6. Original “Bard at Dawn” lute/recorder morning music plays during daytime preparation; a haunting “The Hunt” low-string/bell score takes over at night. Zombie growls accompany nearby undead, owl hoots and crickets sound only at night, and each actual mercenary firing burst triggers a gunshot. **Options → Background music** and **Sound effects** mute independently and remember your choices. Pause, panels, focus loss and backgrounding stop all playback. Woodland silhouettes, worn approach tracks, rocks and abandoned camp debris frame the farm. All sounds and scenery are original and generated locally from source.

The144dp transparent cross and mercenary commands stay pinned above equal-height bottom actions. Matching left/right controls and item slots share uniform spacing; transparent dock padding reveals day/night terrain rather than a dark green slab. Three assignable potion slots surround it; the upper-left slot replaces Merc +. Hold any slot, or tap an empty slot, to assign Elixir, Enhancer, Arc bolt or Starfall. Supplies purchases cost35/45/30/65 coins and stock up to9 each. Arc bolt strikes the nearest threat; Starfall blasts nearby enemies within a0.32 world radius. Damage scales with day/hero training and bosses resist40%. No targets means no potion is spent. Elixir heals35 farm HP,60 surviving mercenary HP and90 hero HP; it is not consumed at full health. Enhancer grants20 active seconds of40% higher damage and20% faster firing, without stacking. All timers pause with the game. They preserve the current play/pause state. The persistent instruction banner and Fix8c label are removed; action feedback remains available to accessibility services, while Help stays in Options. Build has two rows in task order: Fence / Farm / Mill, then Crew / Supplies / Equipment. The Shop map tile opens Farm, and Camp opens Crew. Supplies owns all potion/magic purchases and quick-slot assignment; Equipment owns relics. Switching pages resets to the top, clears stale price feedback and replaces the previous actions; rotation retains the selected page. Building/Build/Squad panels pause simulation. Closing them stays paused; tap Play to resume. Background/focus loss also pauses. Immersive system bars reappear by edge swipe. Native accessible controls match the artwork and have >=48dp targets. Large fonts/short windows can scroll. Seed choice, build panel page and selected border survive rotation. English only; player balance and physical accessibility still need handset validation.

## Century survival

Scouts shuffle slowly and some stand dormant during each six-second daylight phase; no daytime damage. At night, each enemy has an individual shuffling cadence. Ghoul/Ironhide/Wraith join after days15/30/45. Every fifth night, one Grave Warden replaces a reinforcement midway through the quota; it has a distinct large silhouette and a top health bar. Clear all enemies, including the boss, to reach dawn. Boss state saves without duplicating the event. Failure/recovery retries the same day.

Progression scales through Day100, then opens a rift to a fresh world: at most80 enemies,0.6s reinforcement floor, four Ironbound walls with up to20 reinforcement ranks,20 uniform/fortify/drill/hero ranks,10 mill/irrigation tiers. Five-day chapters unlock ranks beyond the early caps; mill/irrigation open every10 days. Existing prices remain linear. Larger later investments improve actual damage/armor/health/output. Survived days means completed nights, displayed prominently above consistently sized HUD buttons. Three mercenaries plus one hero remain the maximum.


## Fantasy survival

One life ended. Your survival story begins. Reincarnate in a world claimed by the dead, build a haven, and survive one hundred days. The Day100 rift starts another world with a fresh farm while retaining relics, worn equipment, button assignments and accessibility/audio preferences. World2 enters **Ashen March**, the first authored second-world biome. Burned pines, kiln ruins, ember-marked heavy foes and ashfall surround an offset settlement cycle. Its four25-day chapters are The Black Pines, Cinder Road, The Silent Kilns and The Regent's Reach. Ashfall slows approaching foes15% while Brutes/Ironhides/Orcs deal20% more damage. Every fifth night brings ashfall and the **Cinder Regent**, a boss variant with10% more health/damage. The century economy, quotas, arenas and relic odds remain. Worlds3+ currently reuse Ashen March; a separate full story campaign is not implemented.

**Equipment** is a dedicated tab, also reachable through Options. Fit Helmet, Boots, Gloves, Weapon, Ring, Necklace, Talisman, Belt and Shoulder pad, or use **Equip best**. Every slot has Common/Uncommon/Rare/Epic/Legendary relics with fixed transparent effects. The45-entry collection deduplicates exact finds; no inventory overflow or automatic sale. Bonuses cap at12% crew/hero attack,12% farm/crew protection,10% magic power and6% attack speed. They do not raise income, range, base health, loot chance or upgrade caps. Relics are optional advantages; farms still need defenses.

Per defeated ordinary zombie, equipment drops at0.4%; bosses12%; arena victory55%. Ordinary drops are at most Rare. Among arena drops,2% are Legendary,14% Epic,49% Rare and35% Uncommon. This means1.1% unconditional Legendary chance per arena win. Duplicate finds do not stack or grant coins. A separate persisted loot RNG leaves enemy/weather randomness unchanged.

After clearing each tenth night, including its boss, an invitation pauses the farm. Accept or decline; Equipment can be changed before entry. One attempt per invitation against one of four random champions: a heavy-blow knight, rapid huntress, slow colossus or pulsing witch. Battles use their own180+ hero resolve, not farm/crew HP; farm clocks and crops freeze. Crew upgrades and worn relics affect your arena avatar, which attacks automatically. **Strike** adds a2.4s cooldown attack, and all three assigned potions/magic work with real shared stock. A45s timeout or defeat ends the attempt without farm damage; retreat grants no reward. There is no replay reward or entry fee. Pausing/backgrounding/rotation freezes and resumes the saved fight. You can remain safely in a completed world before crossing the rift.

**Assign buttons** explicitly lets every one of the three buttons use Elixir, Enhancer, Arc bolt or Starfall interchangeably. Holding a quick slot and tapping an empty slot still work. Assignments never buy or consume stock.

The optional offline **adaptive director** measures raid strain and recent clean nights. It adjusts only future reinforcement spacing, by at most8% slower or6% faster, retaining the0.6s floor. It never changes live enemy health/damage, quotas, bosses, upgrade costs or loot odds, and never guarantees a win. The current pacing state is shown in Equipment; Options disables adaptation. No service, analytics, paid AI or network is used. Human fun/balance validation remains outstanding.

Unsigned.apk is future production-signing output and is not installable directly.

Published TAG builds use one privately retained key, introduced in the0.12 development line, with its public fingerprint pinned in docs/signing-certificate.sha256. Future higher-version APKs signed with that same key install over the app and retain its local checkpoints. Private keys are never committed, cached in public CI or included in releases. Earlier disposable preview keys were not retained; the existing0.11.1 APK cannot be updated by this new key. A one-time export/import is required when moving from those older builds. Do not uninstall until you have exported and kept the backup. **Export your current `.btfs` save before upgrading or uninstalling.** A signing conflict requires export→uninstall→install→import. Uninstall removes local data. Store publication is not configured.

Save schema11 added fishing quota and separate randomness plus nine crop IDs, reads schemas1–10 and retains planted floating beds through dawn. ## Build and test

Use JDK 17, Android SDK platform 35, build-tools 35.0.0, Gradle 8.9 (checksum-pinned wrapper), Android Gradle Plugin 8.7.3. Install Android Studio's command line SDK, accept the licenses, set `ANDROID_HOME` or add `sdk.dir=/absolute/sdk/path` to untracked `local.properties`.

```sh
sdkmanager 'platforms;android-35' 'build-tools;35.0.0' 'platform-tools'
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest testReleaseUnitTest assembleDebug assembleDebugAndroidTest assembleRelease lintDebug lintRelease
python3 scripts/check_package.py "$ANDROID_HOME/build-tools/35.0.0/aapt" app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
```

Outputs: `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk` (test-signed preview), `app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release-unsigned.apk`, and `app/build/outputs/apk/androidTest/debug/app-debug-androidTest.apk`. JVM test reports are under `app/build/reports/tests/testDebugUnitTest/`. First dependency resolution requires internet; the installed game does not.

CI builds and checks each push/PR, then runs native fortress construction/recruitment/pointer, portrait/landscape, 200% font, lifecycle/persistence instrumentation on Android 26 and 35 emulators. A successful authorized main-branch run creates a **prerelease**, never stable, with APKs, source ZIP, SHA-256 checksums, build provenance and test evidence. Existing version tags are never overwritten. See `docs/SPEC.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/TESTING.md`, and `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`.

Zombie designs are original Canvas artwork: ragged walkers, lean runners, armored brutes and bloated spitters, with eyes, teeth, torn clothing and simulation-timed movement. Reduced motion keeps their pose static. Fence-material kit coins buy supplies; timber covers framing. No separate stone/steel inventory is needed.


Every five survived days the settlement changes without resetting assets: Farm(days1–5), Village(days6–10), Castle(days11–15), then repeats. Perimeter shape, building positions, courtyard and surroundings change together. Fences, scouts, mercenaries, heroes and enemy targeting share the same template geometry. Changes occur at dawn, never during a raid. Hero costs360 coins/20 timber; Steel ranks cost120+60×rank coins and18+8×rank timber; Mill/Drill/Hero upgrades show their exact prices in their building popups.

Day/night music now uses original32-second arrangements: bright piano/plucked-string/chord phrases by day and cello ostinato, low strings, piano and heartbeat percussion at night. The scores draw on cinematic suspense conventions and do not copy a film/game theme. Zombie, gunshot and nocturnal sounds remain local/offline.

Crew and Options open an illustrated **Bestiary** with unlock days and tactics.

Arena art now shows a vanguard with cape, steel armor, sword and shield; worn helmet relics alter the headgear. The Hollow Knight, Grave Huntress, Bone Colossus and Ash Witch have distinct armor/anatomy, weapons and magic. A stone gate, heraldry and perspective floor frame the duel.
