# Beyond the Fence

A small farm, a warm home, and something moving past the fence. An offline Android farming and survival game with original vector artwork, quick sessions, and no accounts, ads, purchases or internet permission.

**Current test candidate: 0.12.0-rc.1.** Android 8.0+ (API 26), phones and tablets. Native Java/Canvas; no runtime dependencies. Builds on the verified0.11.1-rc.1 preview;0.12 starts a persistent private signing identity. Baseline rules: [Denberg28/Agents_md v1.1](https://github.com/Denberg28/Agents_md/blob/main/AGENTS.md). The initial rules commit is `1e9f9a0de2f926124d00c8378204c76669db3900`.

## Play · farm fortress

The game is a top-down fortress defense with original, chunky arcade artwork. Crops, a mill, a market and barracks sit inside the fence. The farm occupies a compact central area, leaving visible terrain on every side. Zombies wander outside during preparation, then approach from north, east, south and west during raids. Uniformed mercenaries patrol the perimeter and shoot nearby targets; mercenaries and a recruitable hero fight the horde. One volunteer guard starts with each new farm.

1. Open **Seeds**, select a crop, then tap soil inside the walls. Harvest ripe tiles or use Harvest to earn **coins and timber**. Start with80 coins,24 timber and6 plots; corn/pumpkins become available on days2/3; there are no player levels.
2. The compact, transparent bottom-center **N/W/E/S cross** selects a side and keeps the direct repair action (8 coins, up to50HP, no timber or pause). The center **↑** builds/upgrades only the selected fence, at the usual coin/timber price. It starts disabled until a direction is selected. Unaffordable upgrades stay tappable and briefly show their required coins/timber inside the button, with no charge. Fully maxed upgrades are disabled. Steel gains4 additional reinforcement ranks. Intact-side selection costs nothing. A thin outline marks selection; color and durability bars report damage. East/west start as gaps. Spend coins/timber to build each border, then upgrade **Wood → Stone → Steel**. Durability bars, cracks and numeric HP show remaining fence strength; hearts have been removed. Each fence has independent health. Zombies chip it down and enter when it reaches zero; stronger tiers absorb more damage. Tap a damaged border to repair it for8 coins (+50HP, no timber). Fence also sells10 timber for12 coins. Repair homestead restores the separate farm-core health.
3. Tap an interior **Mill/Shop/Camp** tile to open that building’s own features. Mill provides its construction/repairs and timber bundles. Shop provides market construction/repairs, selling ripe crops, planting the selected crop, irrigation and expansion. Barracks/Camp provides its construction/repairs, recruitment, crew status, rally/patrol, mercenary drills and hero recruitment/training. One hero is separate from the3 mercenary slots; hero death requires paid replacement. Mill adds3 production upgrades; Barracks adds3 drill and3 hero tiers. The timber mill produces4 timber every12 active seconds; the market adds25% to crop sale value; barracks unlock recruitment. All purchases show costs before confirmation by tapping their purchase button.
4. Open **Crew / Barracks** to hire; maximum3 mercenaries. Hiring still requires barracks, the shown coin price and6 timber. They roam the fence even during daylight and shoot zombies within range at night. No recurring wages. Breached zombies can injure and kill mercenaries. Surviving guards heal1.5HP per active preparation second. Rally N/E/S/W moves guards to defend that border; Resume perimeter patrol releases the order. Rally never deals an instant shot.
5. **Merc ↑** beside the cross upgrades all crew uniforms directly without opening a panel; the **Mill / Shop / Barracks** panels also upgrade crew uniforms (Workwear → Scout green → Desert kit → Urban armor → Ranger kit → Black ops), fortress strength, irrigation and farm size. Uniforms visibly change armor, colors and insignia, add3 damage and20 maximum health per tier, improve firing cadence/range and reduce injuries. Each day raises the threat: preparation75→35s, reinforcement3.2→0.85s, quota10→45. Runners rush at twice Walker speed; Brutes are slow, durable fence smashers; Spitters attack from outside the fence. Each killed zombie pays **1 coin**, once; harvests and dawn rewards remain the main income. **Every zombie must be killed to end the raid**; there is no75-second despawn. Rally the short-range crew to threatened borders. Intruders destroy crops and buildings and can wipe out your farm. Defeat offers free recovery at the same day with one volunteer if the squad was lost, keeping surviving assets and upgrades; ruined crops/buildings stay lost. Recovery grants enough seed coins to reach12 if needed.
6. Original “Bard at Dawn” lute/recorder morning music plays during daytime preparation; a haunting “The Hunt” low-string/bell score takes over at night. Zombie growls accompany nearby undead, owl hoots and crickets sound only at night, and each actual mercenary firing burst triggers a gunshot. **Options → Background music** and **Sound effects** mute independently and remember your choices. Pause, panels, focus loss and backgrounding stop all playback. Woodland silhouettes, worn approach tracks, rocks and abandoned camp debris frame the farm. All sounds and scenery are original and generated locally from source.

The144dp transparent cross and mercenary commands stay pinned above equal-height bottom actions. Matching left/right controls and item slots share uniform spacing; transparent dock padding reveals day/night terrain rather than a dark green slab. Three assignable potion slots surround it; the upper-left slot replaces Merc +. Hold any slot, or tap an empty slot, to assign Elixir, Enhancer, Arc bolt or Starfall. Shop purchases cost35/45/30/65 coins and stock up to9 each. Arc bolt strikes the nearest threat; Starfall blasts nearby enemies within a0.32 world radius. Damage scales with day/hero training and bosses resist40%. No targets means no potion is spent. Elixir heals35 farm HP,60 surviving mercenary HP and90 hero HP; it is not consumed at full health. Enhancer grants20 active seconds of40% higher damage and20% faster firing, without stacking. All timers pause with the game. They preserve the current play/pause state. The persistent instruction banner and Fix8c label are removed; action feedback remains available to accessibility services, while Help stays in Options. One consistent Fence / Mill / Shop / Barracks tab strip selects the same pages as the map buildings. Switching pages resets to the top, clears stale price feedback and replaces the previous actions; rotation retains the selected page. Building/Build/Squad panels pause simulation. Closing them stays paused; tap Play to resume. Background/focus loss also pauses. Immersive system bars reappear by edge swipe. Native accessible controls match the artwork and have >=48dp targets. Large fonts/short windows can scroll. Seed choice, build panel page and selected border survive rotation. English only; player balance and physical accessibility still need handset validation.

## Century survival

Scouts shuffle slowly and some stand dormant during each six-second daylight phase; no daytime damage. At night, each enemy has an individual shuffling cadence. Ghoul/Ironhide/Wraith join after days15/30/45. Every fifth night, one Grave Warden replaces a reinforcement midway through the quota; it has a distinct large silhouette and a top health bar. Clear all enemies, including the boss, to reach dawn. Boss state saves without duplicating the event. Failure/recovery retries the same day.

Progression scales through Day100, then remains bounded for endless play: at most80 enemies,0.6s reinforcement floor, four Steel fences with up to20 reinforcement ranks,20 uniform/fortify/drill/hero ranks,10 mill/irrigation tiers. Five-day chapters unlock ranks beyond the early caps; mill/irrigation open every10 days. Existing prices remain linear. Larger later investments improve actual damage/armor/health/output. Survived days means completed nights, displayed prominently above consistently sized HUD buttons. Three mercenaries plus one hero remain the maximum.

## Install / upgrade / rollback

Download **Preview.apk** from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Denberg28/Beyond-the-Fence/releases). Android8.0+; no permissions, accounts, ads or network access. Unsigned.apk is future production-signing output and is not installable directly.

Starting with0.12, published previews use one privately retained key with its public fingerprint pinned in docs/signing-certificate.sha256. Future higher-version APKs signed with that same key install over the app and retain its local checkpoints. Private keys are never committed, cached in public CI or included in releases. Earlier disposable preview keys were not retained; the existing0.11.1 APK cannot be updated by this new key. A one-time export/import is required when moving from those older builds. Do not uninstall until you have exported and kept the backup. **Export your current `.btfs` save before upgrading or uninstalling.** A signing conflict requires export→uninstall→install→import. Uninstall removes local data. Production signing continuity remains unconfigured.

Save schema8 adds three assignable slots, attack-potion inventory and boss state; it reads schema1–7 backups. It also expands validated century ranks/enemy limits. Schema7 added consumables, hero state, drills, mill tiers and per-border reinforcement ranks. It also retains mercenary health. Schema1–6 backups migrate; older combat upgrades become uniforms. Legacy squads above3 retain their first3 guards and receive each excess recruit's original hire cost plus6 timber once (slots4–6:130/155/180 coins). Existing crops, resources, assets and damage remain. Legacy XP is read for compatibility but earns nothing and affects no gameplay. Crop availability and difficulty now depend on day. Schema8 cannot be opened by0.11 or older; keep a pre-upgrade export and previous APK for rollback.

Atomic alternating local checkpoints and validated backup import/export remain. Corrupt imports leave the farm intact; damaged checkpoints recover from the other slot. Keep on-device backups for offline use. No simulation happens while backgrounded or closed.

## Build and test

Use JDK 17, Android SDK platform 35, build-tools 35.0.0, Gradle 8.9 (checksum-pinned wrapper), Android Gradle Plugin 8.7.3. Install Android Studio's command line SDK, accept the licenses, set `ANDROID_HOME` or add `sdk.dir=/absolute/sdk/path` to untracked `local.properties`.

```sh
sdkmanager 'platforms;android-35' 'build-tools;35.0.0' 'platform-tools'
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug assembleDebugAndroidTest assembleRelease lintDebug lintRelease
python3 scripts/check_package.py "$ANDROID_HOME/build-tools/35.0.0/aapt" app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
```

Outputs: `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk` (test-signed preview), `app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release-unsigned.apk`, and `app/build/outputs/apk/androidTest/debug/app-debug-androidTest.apk`. JVM test reports are under `app/build/reports/tests/testDebugUnitTest/`. First dependency resolution requires internet; the installed game does not.

CI builds and checks each push/PR, then runs native fortress construction/recruitment/pointer, portrait/landscape, 200% font, lifecycle/persistence instrumentation on Android 26 and 35 emulators. A successful authorized main-branch run creates a **prerelease**, never stable, with APKs, source ZIP, SHA-256 checksums, build provenance and test evidence. Existing version tags are never overwritten. See `docs/SPEC.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/TESTING.md`, and `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`.

Zombie designs are original Canvas artwork: ragged walkers, lean runners, armored brutes and bloated spitters, with eyes, teeth, torn clothing and simulation-timed movement. Reduced motion keeps their pose static. Fence-material kit coins buy supplies; timber covers framing. No separate stone/steel inventory is needed.


Every five survived days the settlement changes without resetting assets: Farm(days1–5), Village(days6–10), Castle(days11–15), then repeats. Perimeter shape, building positions, courtyard and surroundings change together. Fences, scouts, mercenaries, heroes and enemy targeting share the same template geometry. Changes occur at dawn, never during a raid. Hero costs360 coins/20 timber; Steel ranks cost120+60×rank coins and18+8×rank timber; Mill/Drill/Hero upgrades show their exact prices in their building popups.

Day/night music now uses original32-second arrangements: bright piano/plucked-string/chord phrases by day and cello ostinato, low strings, piano and heartbeat percussion at night. The scores draw on cinematic suspense conventions and do not copy a film/game theme. Zombie, gunshot and nocturnal sounds remain local/offline.
